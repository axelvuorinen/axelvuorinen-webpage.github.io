The course consists of a series of hands-on sessions aimed at introducing first-year undergraduate mathematics students to a range of useful softwares and computing tools for their academic studies.
I was assigned to one of the student groups, where I led the practical exercises carried out in the computer lab.

Syllabus
======
* Lab session 1: Markdown
* Lab session 2: LibreOffice Writer
* Lab session 3: LaTeX
* Lab session 4: Beamer
* Lab session 5: LibreOffice Calc
* Lab session 6: Effective algebra (SageMath, Python)
