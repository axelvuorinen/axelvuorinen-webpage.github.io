This undergraduate-level course (3rd year) introduced mathematics students to key concepts in statistics and probabilistic simulations. Topics included simulation of random variables, estimation theory, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing.
I was responsible for teaching exercise sessions and practical computer classes for one of the assigned student groups.

Syllabus
======
* Simulation of real-valued random variables
* Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem, Markov and Chebyshev inequalities
* __Statistical estimation__: estimators, bias, convergence, quadratic risk
* Asymptotic properties and normal approximations
* Method of moments and maximum likelihood estimation
* Confidence intervals and hypothesis testing
