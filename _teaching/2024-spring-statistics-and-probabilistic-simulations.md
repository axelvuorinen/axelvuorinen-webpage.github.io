This undergraduate-level course introduces 3rd-year mathematics students to key concepts in statistics and probabilistic simulations. Topics included simulation of random variables, estimation theory, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing.
For one of the student groups, I was responsible for leading tutorial sessions to practice exercises and for conducting computer lab classes to teach statistical inference using R.

Syllabus
======
* Simulation of real-valued random variables
* Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem, Markov and Chebyshev inequalities
* __Statistical estimation__: estimators, bias, convergence, quadratic risk
* Asymptotic properties and normal approximations
* Method of moments and maximum likelihood estimation
* Confidence intervals and hypothesis testing
* Introduction to R and fundamental tools for statistical inference
